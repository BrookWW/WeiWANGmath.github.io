About Me

I am a fifth-year Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University, supervised by Prof. Zhifei Zhang. Previously, I obtained my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Zhejiang University in 2022, where I was advised by Prof. Ting Zhang.

I co-organize long-term analysis seminars at Peking University, focusing on PDEs and geometric measure theory.
